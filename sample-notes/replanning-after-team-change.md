# Replanning after a reduction in team size

## First week

Do not pretend the roadmap is unchanged. Within the first week, tell
stakeholders plainly that commitments will be re-planned and give a date for
the new plan. Silence gets filled with assumptions that everything is still on
track.

## Triage the roadmap

Sort every commitment into three groups: keep (contractual or regulatory, or
already more than 80 percent done), defer (valuable but movable), and stop.
The stop list is the one that matters, and it must be explicit and agreed with
product. Work that is merely not mentioned keeps consuming attention.

## Rebuild capacity honestly

Recalculate capacity from the people actually on the team, then take another
20 percent off for the first two sprints. People are absorbing new areas,
covering on-call gaps and processing what happened. Planning at the old
velocity guarantees a second round of missed commitments.

## Knowledge gaps

List every system that lost its primary owner. Assign a new owner within two
weeks and schedule a recorded walkthrough while the context still exists.

## The team

Say what you know, what you do not know, and when you will know more. Hold
one-on-ones more often for a month, not less.
