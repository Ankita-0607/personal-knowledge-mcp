# General management programme: notes

## Unit economics

Contribution margin per unit is price minus variable cost. A business scales
when contribution margin is positive and customer lifetime value is comfortably
above customer acquisition cost; a common rule of thumb is a ratio of at least
three to one, with the acquisition cost paid back within twelve months.

## Competitive strategy

Porter's five forces: rivalry among existing competitors, the threat of new
entrants, the threat of substitutes, the bargaining power of buyers and the
bargaining power of suppliers. The framework explains industry profitability,
not which company wins inside the industry.

## Strategy is choice

A strategy says what we will not do. A list of everything that would be nice is
a wish list. The test: would a sensible competitor choose the opposite? If
nobody would, it is not a strategic choice.

## Reading financial statements

The income statement shows profitability over a period, the balance sheet shows
position on a date, and the cash-flow statement reconciles the two. Profitable
companies fail when they run out of cash, which is why working capital matters.

## Negotiation

Know your best alternative to a negotiated agreement before you sit down, and
estimate theirs. The party with the better alternative has the leverage.
Negotiate on interests, not positions.
