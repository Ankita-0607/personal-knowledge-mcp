# Hiring loop for engineers

## Structure

Four interviews after the recruiter screen: a practical coding exercise in the
candidate's own editor, a system design conversation, a code review exercise
where they review a real pull request with planted problems, and a values and
collaboration interview with someone outside the hiring team.

## Scorecards

Every interviewer scores against the same written rubric and submits their
scorecard before the debrief, without seeing anyone else's. This stops the
first confident opinion in the room from anchoring everyone else.

## Debrief

Held within 24 hours of the last interview. The hiring manager speaks last. We
decide hire or no hire; "maybe" means no. Each interviewer states the strongest
evidence for and against.

## What we stopped doing

Whiteboard algorithm puzzles and take-home tests longer than two hours. Both
filtered for free time and interview practice rather than for the work.

## Candidate experience

Every candidate hears back within three working days of their final interview,
and everyone who reached the on-site stage gets specific feedback if they ask.
