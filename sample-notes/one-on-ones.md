# Running one-on-ones

## Cadence

Weekly, 30 minutes, for every direct report. Fortnightly is acceptable only for
very senior people who ask for it. Never cancel; move it. A cancelled one-on-one
tells the person they are the lowest priority on my calendar.

## Agenda

It is their meeting, not mine. They bring the topics in a shared running
document and we start with their list. I keep at most three items of my own and
raise them in the last ten minutes. Status updates are banned: status belongs in
the tracker and the standup.

## Questions that open people up

- What is taking more energy than it should right now?
- What would you change about how the team works if it were entirely up to you?
- What is one thing I could do differently that would make your week easier?

## Career conversations

Once a quarter the whole slot is about growth, not current work. We review the
growth plan, pick one skill to stretch, and I commit to finding one concrete
opportunity for it before the next quarterly conversation.

## Notes and follow-up

I write down commitments I made during the meeting and open the next one by
reporting on them. Trust is built by the manager closing their own loops first.
